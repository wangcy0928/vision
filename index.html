Starting

Vision

  • Walk. Beeps warn you about things in your way: left ear "tok", right ear "ting", ahead "bzz". Faster = closer.
  • Point at something and hold still, or tap, to hear what it is.
  • Hold the screen and ask anything: "Read this", "What's in front of me?"
  • Say "Find the door", then turn in a circle and follow the beeps to it. Say "stop" to end.
